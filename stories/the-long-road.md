# Book I — Dust

The dunes did not end. They only changed color — from copper to ash, from ash to a pale that refused a name.

## Act 1 — Departure

### Chapter 1 — The Leaving

She packed little: water, a knife, a letter she would not open until the sky remembered blue. The village watched her go without waving. That was kinder.

By noon the first dune had already erased the path behind her.

### Chapter 2 — The Middle Miles

#### Copper

Heat shimmered like a second horizon. Lizards stitched the sand with quick stitches and vanished. She counted steps until counting became breathing, and breathing became enough.

#### Ash

Night arrived without stars worth naming. She slept in the lee of a ridge and dreamed of rooms with doors that opened onto other rooms, never the road.

## Act 2 — Enough

### Chapter 3 — Horizon

Somewhere ahead, the sky would remember how to be blue. Until then, the road was enough.
